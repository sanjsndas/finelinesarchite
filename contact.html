    Before You Write

    How do I pitch a story idea?

    Send a concise pitch (200–300 words) outlining the angle, why it matters now, and your proposed sources or case studies. Please include links to previous writing samples if available. We review pitches on a rolling basis.

    Do you accept guest posts?

    Occasionally. We prioritize pitches from architects, designers, and researchers with direct industry experience. We do not accept pre-written articles or sponsored posts disguised as editorial.

    How quickly will I hear back?

    Our editorial team is small. We aim to respond to all pitches and inquiries within two business days. If you haven't heard from us after a week, feel free to follow up with a brief reminder.

    Can I republish your content?

    We hold copyright to all original content. If you'd like to republish an excerpt (up to 150 words) with a link back to the full article, please request permission first. Contact hello@finelinesarchitecture.com.
